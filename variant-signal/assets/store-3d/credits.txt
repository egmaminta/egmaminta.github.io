The three clips, posters and depth sequences come from DexYCB subject 10, recording 20201022_110843, frames 0–71 inclusive. The views use cameras 932122061900 (side), 932122060861 (overhead) and 836212060125 (front). RGB frames were cropped, lightly brightened and encoded. Aligned depth was downsampled and quantized. The recording shows one participant lifting a Master Chef coffee can in the DexYCB laboratory. It illustrates a grasp, independently of the GroceryVision experiments.
