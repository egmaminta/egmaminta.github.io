Artificial Intelligence · Ph.D. student

Emmanuel G. Maminta.

University of the Philippines Diliman

Metro Manila, Philippines

I am a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at the Ubiquitous Computing Laboratory, advised by Prof. Rowel O. Atienza. I currently work as an AI Developer at OpenText Philippines.

My research focuses on embodied AI, multimodal AI, and vision-language models (VLMs), with a strong emphasis on retail applications (RetailVision). My broader interests include hand–object interaction, egocentric and exocentric computer vision, robotics, and world models.

Retail applications

Research

01

Multimodal AI & VLMs

Vision-language models and multimodal learning for fine-grained grocery product retrieval and open-vocabulary recognition in retail environments.

02

Embodied AI

Agents that perceive, reason, and act in retail environments, with interests in robotics, world models, and simulation.

03

Hand–object interaction

Hand–object interaction and visual understanding from egocentric and exocentric perspectives, especially in retail settings.

Applied work

My work focuses on parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT), agentic AI, and harness engineering.

Additional interests: resource-efficient pre-training, reinforcement learning for LLMs, reward modeling, preference learning, AI safety and alignment, chain-of-thought monitoring, and prompt optimization.
