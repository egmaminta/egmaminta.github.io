Emmanuel G. Maminta

I am a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at the University of the Philippines Diliman, working in the Ubiquitous Computing Laboratory with Prof. Rowel O. Atienza.

My research focuses on embodied AI, multimodal AI, and vision-language models for retail applications (RetailVision, Physical AI Retail). Beyond my primary research focus, I am studying hand–object interaction, egocentric and exocentric computer vision, robotics, and world models.

In September 2026, I joined the Cohere Labs Open Science Community, where my focus is on multimodal AI and world models.

I also work as an AI Developer at OpenText Philippines, building enterprise AI products, agentic systems, and LLMOps/MLOps infrastructure. My applied work includes parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) and harness engineering.
